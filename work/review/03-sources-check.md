# 03 — Sources and fact check (VeriLens prep report)

Checked: 2026-11-21. Method: every URL entry was found through WebSearch and then opened with WebFetch. Entries without a URL were confirmed through search. Each `\cite` in `chapters/*.tex` and `figures/fig-timeline.tex` was compared with the cited page. No project files were edited.

**Verdict:** Every source exists and most numbers are correct. Six changes are needed before submission. (1) The NewsGuard figures (3,006, 300–500 a month, 141 brands) are not in the cited Decoder article. (2) Nikon is listed as a camera that signs C2PA today, but the same source says Nikon's signing was suspended. (3) The "shared as real by public officials" claim about the Hurricane Helene image is not in the VERIFY article. (4) The four-element deepfake test comes from the Commission's guidelines, not from Article 50 itself. (5) The Tech Insider funding figures could not be read at the source. (6) The bib metadata for `shipley`, `time2024` and `newsguard2026` is wrong.

## Unsupported or wrong claims (with suggested corrections)

1. **chapters/ch04-literature.tex:41.** "3,006", "300 to 500 a month" and "141 brands over two months" are attributed to `newsguard2026` (The Decoder), which says only "more than 3,000" and "hundreds ... every month" and does not mention the 141 brands. **Fix:** add a bib entry for the NewsGuard press release of 12 Mar 2026 and cite it here, or keep The Decoder and write "more than 3,000 ... hundreds a month" and drop the 141 figure. In the same entry, set the author to Bastian, M., with the organization as The Decoder.
2. **chapters/ch01-exec.tex:7.** "In March 2026 3,006 sites were operating" `\cite{newsguard2026}`: the same problem. Cite the NewsGuard release, or write "more than 3,000 identified".
3. **chapters/ch02-intro.tex:51 and chapters/ch04-literature.tex:17.** Nikon is listed among cameras that "sign today". The cited source says Nikon's C2PA signing was suspended and its certificates revoked, and not restored as of early 2026. ch04:19 itself mentions the suspension. **Fix:** remove Nikon from the "signing today" lists, or add "(Nikon: suspended)". Also add `\cite{c2pa2026}` to the uncited suspension sentence at ch04:19 and name the model (Nikon Z6 III). Optionally note that Sony's support is through the PXW-Z300 camcorder.
4. **chapters/ch04-literature.tex:85.** The "girl with puppy" image "was shared as real even by public officials" `\cite{verify2024}`: the VERIFY article names no official. **Fix:** add a source such as Digital Camera World, "Politicians distraught over AI image of child and puppy caught in Hurricane Helene" (L. Helm, 9 Oct 2024), which names Sen. Mike Lee and Amy Kremer.
5. **chapters/ch02-intro.tex:50 and chapters/ch04-literature.tex:56.** The four-element deepfake test is attributed to Article 50 or the Act. Per `euaiact2026` it comes from the Commission's (draft) **guidelines** on Article 50. **Fix:** write "according to the Commission's guidelines on Article 50 ...". The grace period at ch04:14 is correct; you could add that it applies only to machine-readable marking.
6. **chapters/ch03-problem.tex:10.** Detectors "lose accuracy when the image is compressed or downscaled" `\cite{ren2025}`: the source shows degradation from **super-resolution** (upscaling). **Fix:** write "after upscaling (super-resolution)", or cite a source that covers compression.
7. **chapters/ch04-literature.tex:99–101 and :108.** The funding figures (~33M, ~120M, ~3.2M) and "none has a published newsroom use case" could not be checked at `techinsider2026`, because the page would not load. Only Reality Defender's $33M is confirmed independently. **Fix:** check the page by hand, or cite primary funding announcements. Until then, mark these figures "according to the review".
8. **chapters/ch15-business.tex:32.** "TrueMedia closed after less than a year" is right, but the cited article does not say it, and the sentence leaves out that the tool was revived at Georgetown. **Fix:** add the GeekWire shutdown article (Jan 2025) or TrueMedia's post of 7 Jan 2025, and write "closed in January 2025 after about nine months, and was later taken over by Georgetown University".
9. **chapters/ch04-literature.tex:108.** The CJR "layer of confirmation" line is a quote from Emmanuelle Saliba about GetReal. **Fix:** write "a verification editor quoted by CJR describes the tool as one 'layer of confirmation' ...".
10. **chapters/ch04-literature.tex:108 (Clarity).** "Moved to workforce identity verification" overstates the source. **Fix:** write "and its technology is being integrated into Deel's workforce identity checks".
11. **chapters/ch04-literature.tex:39.** "1.9 million תגובות" should be "1.9 million interactions (אינטראקציות)".
12. **figures/fig-timeline.tex:7.** The Facebook spam case is dated 2024-04. The paper was published Aug 2024 (preprint Mar 2024). **Fix:** use 2024-08 for publication, or label it "data as of Apr 2024".
13. **chapters/ch01-exec.tex:49.** "The European labelling duty applies from August 2026" sits in a sentence that cites only `c2pa2026`, which does not cover the EU law. **Fix:** add `\cite{euaiact2026}`.
14. **chapters/ch07-alternatives.tex:8.** "Rejects about 99%" is inferred from "<1%". **Fix:** write "more than 99%".
